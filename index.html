75%

Know exactly how many classes you can skip.

Most colleges need 75% attendance to sit in exams. Enter your classes held and attended, and The Lecture Ledger tells you how many more you can miss, or how many you must attend to recover.

Quick check

How it works

Attendance % = (classes attended / classes held) x 100.

If you are above the limit, the site finds how many extra classes you can miss. If you are below, it finds how many classes in a row you must attend. Use the Subject Tracker to check every subject together.
